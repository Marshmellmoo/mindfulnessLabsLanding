What is Mindfulness Labs?
Mindfulness Labs is a P-12 mental health and well-being platform built by Effective to Great Education (ETGE). It helps schools make well-being foundational to learning through evidence-based supports for mental health, wellness, and school climate. The platform is designed for educators, school mental health teams, and school leadership.
What does Mindfulness Labs do?
Mindfulness Labs gives schools the tools to measure, teach, and strengthen well-being across their school community. That includes real-time monitoring of student and staff wellness, AI-supported lesson content, educator professional development, restorative justice practices, emotional regulation curriculum, and culturally responsive resources, all built on evidence-based frameworks.
Who is Mindfulness Labs designed for?
Mindfulness Labs is designed for educators, school mental health professionals, and school leadership. The platform serves the entire school community, including students, staff, and families.
What grade levels does Mindfulness Labs serve?
Mindfulness Labs serves P-12 schools, from pre-K through 12th grade.
Has Mindfulness Labs been tested in real schools?
Yes. Mindfulness Labs has been piloted with 250 or more students, 30 or more educators, and 15 or more school mental health professionals across six school communities. The program was developed in partnership with educators, families, students, and university researchers.
What is the website for Mindfulness Labs?
You can learn more at www.mindfulnesslabs.ai or reach us at hello@effectivetogreat.com.
Who created Mindfulness Labs?
Mindfulness Labs was created by Effective to Great Education (ETGE), an ed-tech organization focused on P-12 mental health and well-being solutions.

Chatbot Response
What does the Mindfulness Labs platform include?
The Mindfulness Labs platform includes six core capability areas: School Wellness Management, Measuring School Community Well-Being, Learning Mental Health and Well-Being, Engaging Restorative Justice, Engaging Mental and Physical Well-Being, and Engaging Emotional Regulation. Together these give schools a complete system for supporting student and staff wellness.
What is School Wellness Management?
School Wellness Management is a prevention and management system that assesses school community well-being in real time through co-creation. It provides targeted guidance to improve and maintain school community wellness in a culturally responsive, trauma-informed way that is inclusive of Black, Brown, and Indigenous experience.
How does Mindfulness Labs measure well-being?
The platform includes Monitoring and Screening tools that track feelings of students, staff, and families, as well as truancy, behavior growth, and academic performance. This gives school leaders real-time insight into how their school community is doing.
What curriculum and content does Mindfulness Labs provide?
The platform offers curriculum and content resources covering mindfulness, mental health, basic neuroscience, and healthy foods. It also includes activities and programs in mindfulness, restorative justice, social-emotional learning, and Multi-Tiered System Support (MTSS).
Does Mindfulness Labs support educator professional development?
Yes. The platform includes Educator Training and Professional Development focused on mental health, helping educators build knowledge and skills for their own well-being and for better supporting their students.
What is the Restorative Justice component?
The Engage Restorative Justice component provides an anti-bullying and violence prevention framework and practices to help schools build safer, more supportive communities.
What does the Mental and Physical Well-Being component cover?
This component offers holistic wellness practices including yoga, fitness, breathing, nutrition, affirmation, meditation, connection with nature, and building healthy environments for both students and educators.
What is the Emotional Regulation component?
The Engage Emotional Regulation component helps students develop skills in emotional literacy, mindfulness, self-awareness, social awareness, relationships, and decision-making so they can consistently manage their emotional and mental well-being.
Does Mindfulness Labs use AI?
Yes. The platform uses AI-supported lessons and resources that integrate well-being into everyday learning for students and educators.
Is Mindfulness Labs culturally responsive?
Yes. Cultural responsiveness is a core part of the platform. Mindfulness Labs includes deeply inclusive mental health resources designed to support health self-identity and the affirmation of historically marginalized communities, including Black, Brown, and Indigenous students and families.

What are the main benefits of Mindfulness Labs?
The platform delivers four core benefits: Student Well-Being, Educator Support, School Climate Insights, and MTSS and PBIS Alignment.
How does Mindfulness Labs support student well-being?
Mindfulness Labs gives students access to evidence-based wellness content, emotional regulation tools, and culturally responsive resources that help them manage stress, build self-awareness, and stay engaged in learning.
How does it support educators?
The platform provides educators with AI-supported lesson content, professional development on mental health, and tools to support their own well-being. When educators are supported, they are better equipped to support their students.
What are School Climate Insights?
School Climate Insights are real-time data and trends on well-being across the school community, covering students, staff, and families. This helps school leaders make informed decisions about where to focus support.
What does MTSS and PBIS Alignment mean?
Mindfulness Labs is designed to align with Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS) and Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS), which are two of the most widely used school-wide frameworks in the country. This means schools can implement Mindfulness Labs without having to rebuild or replace existing systems.
What evidence supports Mindfulness Labs?
The platform is built on evidence-based practices and was developed in partnership with university researchers. It aligns with MTSS, PBIS, trauma-informed care, and culturally responsive frameworks, all of which are grounded in research on what works in school wellness.
How does Mindfulness Labs strengthen school climate?
By supporting emotional health, belonging, and engagement across the school community, and by providing tools for restorative justice and anti-bullying practices, Mindfulness Labs helps schools build an environment where students and staff feel safe, seen, and supported.
